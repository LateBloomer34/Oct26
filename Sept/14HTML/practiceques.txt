Q14. — Debugging challenge

<html>

<head>
    <title>My Page</title>
</head>

<body>

    <h1>My Website</h1>

    <p>This is my <strong>first website.</p>

Find all important problems you can identify.

Don't just fix the code.

Explain why each issue matters.
