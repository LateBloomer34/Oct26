☐ 101. Two Sum — Brute Force

☐ 103. Unique Pairs with Target Sum

☐ 104. Count Pairs with Target Sum

☐ 105. Three Sum

☐ 106. Three Sum Closest
Input:  arr = [-1, 2, 1, -4], target = 1

☐ 107. Four Sum

☐ 110. Maximum Pair Sum Not Exceeding Target

☐ 113. Equilibrium Index

☐ 114. Pivot Index
